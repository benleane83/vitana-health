## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** This does not immediately read as generic AI output. The restrained indigo palette, flat tonal surfaces, explicit evidence metadata, and consistent humanist type are aligned with the product. Product-slop remains in the pill-heavy vocabulary, decorative radial canvas washes, generic eyebrow, and one-long-panel composition. More importantly, the interface looks trustworthy while core evidence and controls are not trustworthy; that mismatch is more damaging than visual cliché.

**Deterministic scan:** The CLI detector returned 45 findings: 42 `design-system-color`, one `design-system-font`, one `design-system-radius`, and one `layout-transition`; 43 were advisory and two warning severity. Forty findings were in `apps/web/src/styles.css` and five in `apps/web/src/styles/a11y.css`. Most color findings are low-value drift reports over illustration colors, alpha variants, accessibility treatments, provider badges, and the documented dialog shadow. The `SFMono-Regular` CSV font is functionally appropriate despite not being tokenized. Actionable findings are the width transition, a 39px field-label overflow, and cramped table-scroll content.

**Visual overlays:** Mutable injection succeeded in a fresh browser tab. The detector overlay reported `cramped-padding` on `.query-table-scroll`, `text-overflow` on the Date field, `single-font`, `flat-type-hierarchy`, and `layout-transition`. The single-font and compact type-ratio flags are false positives against the product register. The overlay also confirmed the form extends past the viewport. The live-server process was stopped, the port was closed, and the page was reloaded to remove the injected overlay.

## Overall Impression

The app has a credible visual foundation but currently fails the emotional contract of a private health evidence workspace. The single biggest opportunity is to put data normalization, plausibility, and quality state ahead of presentation polish so the calm interface never gives malformed evidence unearned authority.

## What's Working

- Evidence basics are easy to scan: value, unit, timestamp, source, record count, and reference range are close to the decision they support.
- Loading skeletons, accessible chart-point names, visible focus treatment, and text-backed status patterns show real accessibility intent.
- The cool canvas, restrained indigo selection, flat borders, and consistent spacing fit repeated personal-data review without hospital severity or wellness-game optimism.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Physiologically impossible oxygen values are presented as trusted evidence

**Why it matters:** `9300%` beside a `92–100%` range can make a user believe either their health is in crisis or the entire local record is corrupt. The chart then compounds the error with a meaningless scale.

**Fix:** Normalize Health Connect percentage semantics exactly once at the ingestion boundary, validate plausible ranges and units before persistence/display, and attach a visible quality state when a record cannot be trusted. Add regression coverage using representative Health Connect oxygen records.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden apps/web`

### [P1] Visible chart and manual-entry controls have an incomplete component contract

**Why it matters:** `ObservationTypeDetailPage` requires chart-range, chart-mode, manual-add, and default-unit props, while `TrackRoute` does not supply them. Clicking a visible range control can throw `onRangeChange is not a function`, violating the expectation that standard controls work.

**Fix:** Wire every required callback and unit source through the route, or hide unfinished controls until their behavior exists. Add a focused interaction test that changes chart range/mode and submits a valid manual observation.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden apps/web`

### [P1] The trust-critical quality and safety layer is missing

**Why it matters:** The overview gives source and recency but no normalized/invalid/duplicate/stale state, no explanation of what the reference range means, and no boundary on what the user should conclude. Calm styling makes questionable values look authoritative.

**Fix:** Place a compact evidence-quality row next to the latest reading and chart: normalized unit, source, observed/imported time, duplicate/validation state, and a plain-language interpretation boundary. Invalid evidence should be visibly quarantined from trend summaries with a direct correction path.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify apps/web`

### [P1] Manual entry overflows at a common desktop/tablet width

**Why it matters:** At roughly 921px, the six-column form extends the page to about 975px and pushes the submit action partly off-screen. This is a direct usability failure at a normal window width.

**Fix:** Add an intermediate structural breakpoint: split the intro across the full row, use two or three field columns, let Note span remaining width, and keep the action visible. Preserve 44px targets and verify at 320px, 768px, 921px, and wide desktop.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt apps/web`

### [P2] Evidence review, destructive maintenance, and data entry compete on one long surface

**Why it matters:** The trend toolbar, 75-row table, bulk delete, repeated row actions, pagination, and add form create six failed cognitive-load checks. Keyboard users face dozens of chart points and row actions before reaching later tasks.

**Fix:** Make evidence review the default mode. Show a recent, bounded entry set with search/filtering; move maintenance actions into a clearly entered mode; progressively disclose manual entry. Keep bulk deletion visually separate from ordinary review.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill apps/web`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan, first-time health-data reviewer:** The path begins calmly, then presents `9300%`, `oxygen_saturation`, `Smart`, and `Raw` without explanation. Jordan cannot tell whether the reading is dangerous, malformed, or simply stored in a different unit, and receives no nearby recovery guidance.

**Sam, keyboard and screen-reader user:** The global `role="tablist"` uses roving `tabIndex` but lacks arrow-key navigation, leaving inactive tabs out of sequential focus. The detail surface then exposes many chart points and up to two actions per row, creating an exhausting focus sequence without a shortcut to review, maintenance, or entry modes.

**Riley, privacy-conscious power user:** Provenance is present, but duplicate readings, free-text units, implausible percentages, stale cached data, and implementation-flavored source labels prevent Riley from auditing whether the evidence is normalized and current.

## Cognitive Load

Six of eight checks fail: single focus, chunking, visual hierarchy, one thing at a time, minimal choices, and progressive disclosure. Grouping and working-memory support pass. Decision points above four visible options include the global navigation, six trend controls, the large entry set, and the manual-entry area. The primary task should be narrowed to reviewing evidence, with maintenance and entry explicitly entered when needed.

## Emotional Journey

The opening is composed and credible, with the latest value, date, source, and range visible. The emotional valley arrives when an impossible percentage is presented without a quality warning; the interface offers no reassurance about whether the problem is the body, the import, or the app. Destructive deletion receives confirmation, but anomalous evidence receives less guidance. The page ends in an unconstrained entry form rather than restoring confidence with a clear validation or next step.

## Minor Observations

- The agents observed different console states: one saw repeated API `401`s plus the range-control exception; the isolated detector pass saw no console errors after reload. Treat authentication/degraded-state messaging as a scenario to reproduce, not a settled always-on defect.
- The generic eyebrow `Loaded health data by type` adds little and contributes to template-like hierarchy.
- The settings text glyph is the only icon-only global destination and weakens navigation consistency.
- `Health Connect (android-companion)` exposes an implementation identifier rather than polished provenance language.
- The pale back-link treatment deserves a measured AA contrast check rather than visual assumption.
- The detector's `single-font` and `flat-type-hierarchy` findings conflict with the product register and should be ignored for this surface.

## Questions to Consider

- Should any physiological value reach the primary evidence surface before unit normalization and plausibility validation succeed?
- After an anomalous reading, does the user need another chart control first, or a concise explanation of source, quality, and what not to conclude?
- Should `Review evidence`, `Maintain records`, and `Add measurement` be separate task modes instead of one uninterrupted page?
- What is the intended efficient keyboard path through dozens of observations without visiting every chart point and row action?
